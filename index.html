Knowing When to Act: A Hybrid Classification– Generation System for Proactive Audio Engagement

CS 5814 Course Project, Fall 2026

View Project Proposal (PDF)

Abstract

Audio-aware assistants in homes, workplaces, and care settings must decide not only what they hear but whether responding would help. We propose a system for Track II (Proactive Audio Engagement) of the HEARTBEAT Challenge, in which a system hears 10–90s of environmental or human non-speech audio and must choose among silence, monitor, ask, and intervene, decide when to engage, and produce a concrete response with a brief rationale. Our approach decouples deciding from speaking: a frozen pretrained audio encoder feeds a lightweight causal temporal model that predicts actions frame by frame and derives the engagement time, while a compact language model fine-tuned with LoRA generates the response conditioned on the predicted action and detected sound events. A template-based generator serves as an internal baseline, allowing a controlled comparison of retrieval versus generation. We will evaluate on the challenge development data and leaderboard, with particular attention to false activations and robustness under distribution shift.
